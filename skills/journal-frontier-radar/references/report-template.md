# Journal frontier report template

## 1. Scope and coverage

- Journal identity, publisher, ISSN, official URL.
- Exact inclusive dates and publication-date rule.
- Included and excluded article types.
- Included article count.
- Full-text, abstract-only, and metadata-only counts and percentages.
- Discovery sources, archive totals, unresolved discrepancies, and audit status.

## 2. Executive findings

State 3–7 conclusions. Label each as journal-attention finding, field-frontier finding, or interpretation.

## 3. Topic landscape

For each topic include count, corpus share, article-type mix, representative articles, principal methods/models, and evidence strength.

## 4. Keyword map

Group keywords into scientific concepts, methods/technologies, populations/models, outcomes, and translational contexts. Give document frequency and explain synonym merges.

## 5. Temporal momentum

Compare normalized topic shares in the earlier and later halves of the window. Identify rising, stable, and declining themes without over-interpreting small counts.

## 6. What the journal is emphasizing

Explain editorial or scientific attention visible in the corpus. Distinguish special-issue effects, review-heavy topics, and genuine growth in primary research.

## 7. State of the broader frontier

For each major topic:

- Established advances.
- Emerging advances.
- Contested or negative evidence.
- Technical and conceptual bottlenecks.
- How the broader field differs from the target journal's emphasis.

## 8. Open scientific questions

For each question provide evidence basis, gap type, why unresolved, proposed design, primary endpoint/falsification criterion, dependencies, risks, impact, tractability, and time horizon.

## 9. Research opportunities

Prioritize opportunities in a compact matrix: significance, novelty, feasibility, required data/resources, and likely failure modes.

## 10. Limitations

Discuss access restrictions, abstract-only evidence, index mismatch, date ambiguity, language bias, topic-label subjectivity, and limitations of the selected time window.

## Appendix A. Complete article inventory

List every included article with ID, title, date, type, DOI/URL, reading level, topics, and keywords.

## Appendix B. Exclusions and discrepancies

List excluded records, duplicate merges, boundary decisions, corrections/retractions, and unresolved index discrepancies.

## Appendix C. Frontier evidence

List every external frontier source and the claim it supports.
